Using the event portal

Terms of Service

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The Manhunt IX organisers run this portal for the group challenge in Singapore on 13 November 2026. These terms explain how to use it. The Privacy Policy explains what personal data we use and why.

Accounts and access

Provide accurate information when requesting an account, keep your sign-in details private, and use only the account assigned to you. Player access requires organiser approval. Organisers manage account access, groups, invitations, event records, and submission reviews.

If you are under 13, ask your parent or legal guardian to contact us. Wait until we confirm their consent before you sign up or send personal data. If you are unsure what sharing your data means, ask a parent or guardian to help you read the Privacy Policy.

Do not use another person's account or invitation without permission, attempt to access another group's private information, or interfere with the portal or its security.

Event participation and conduct

Follow instructions from event organisers and applicable event rules. Use good judgment and take care of yourself and other people while participating. The portal is for recording and reviewing event activity; submitting an item does not itself mean that it has been approved or that a task is complete. Organisers make those decisions.

Submissions and other material

You keep your rights to the material you submit. You give the organisers permission to store, display and review it only as needed to run the event, check results and keep necessary event records. This includes showing relevant submissions to your group and authorised reviewers. Any personal data in the material is handled under the Privacy Policy. These terms do not give the organisers permission to publish your evidence or use it for promotion.

Share only what the task needs. Before uploading anyone else’s image, voice or other identifying details, explain that your group and authorised organisers can see the evidence and get that person’s permission. If it identifies a child under 13, contact us first and wait until we confirm consent from their parent or legal guardian. Keep bystanders and unrelated private details out of the shot, and remember that original files may contain location details. Do not submit unlawful, threatening, harassing or unrelated material.

Keep invitation links private. Enter someone else’s email address to send an invitation only if they have agreed to be invited. Do not post or forward another person’s evidence or personal details outside the portal without their permission.

Portal messages and notifications

Organisers may use the portal to provide task feedback and event announcements. If you choose to provide a verified contact email or enable device notifications, relevant messages may also be sent through those channels. You can manage contact addresses and turn off device notifications in Settings. Delivery depends on the email or notification provider and may be delayed or unavailable.

Availability and changes

We work to keep the portal useful during the event, but it may be interrupted for maintenance, technical problems, or circumstances outside the organisers’ control. Keep your own copy of any material you cannot easily replace. The portal and its features may change as needed for event operations.

Access may be limited

Organisers may temporarily limit or remove portal access when reasonably needed to protect participants, address misuse, enforce event rules, or maintain the service. Where practical, they will explain the reason and let you contact them about the decision.

Contact

Questions about these terms or use of the portal can be sent to noreply.manhunt@gmail.com.

For privacy questions, complaints or requests about your data, see the data protection contact. Accepting these terms does not give blanket consent to other uses of your data or limit your rights under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012.
