Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The Manhunt IX organisers run this portal for the event in Singapore. This policy explains what personal data we handle, why we need it, who can see it, and how to contact us under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

Your group and authorised organisers can see the evidence you upload. An original photo or video can contain location details. You can contact us about your data even if you don’t have a portal account.

What we collect and why

  • Registration and account approval. We use your name, username, required registration phone number, account role and approval status to set up and review your account request. Only authorised administrators can see your phone number. You can register with a username and password without providing an email address. If you provide an email address at registration, we may use it to send an approval notice.
  • Sign-in and account security. We keep protected records for your chosen sign-in methods, such as a password hash, sign-in sessions, verified email addresses and, if enabled, authenticator and backup-code records. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive your Google account ID, profile name and verified email to sign you in and keep the right account linked. We do not receive your Google password or read your inbox.
  • Groups and invitations. We use group names, rosters, membership records and invitation details to manage groups. An organiser or group leader can provide your email address to send you an invitation. Other players may give us your name when setting up a group.
  • Event activity and evidence. We use task progress, notes, submissions, reviews, feedback and game activity to run the event and check results. For the map challenge, we keep your submitted Google Maps link and pin coordinates with your guess. The portal does not track your live location. We store uploaded files in their original form, along with file names, upload times, and any capture details your browser sends. Photos and videos may show someone’s face or capture their voice, and original files can contain details such as when or where they were taken. Organisers may check those details when reviewing evidence. Images may have a smaller preview.
  • Messages. We use the portal inbox, verified contact addresses and, if you turn on device notifications, your device’s push subscription to deliver event messages, task feedback and alerts. We also use contact details and the information you give us when responding to support or privacy requests.
  • Technical records. We and our providers may use request information such as IP addresses, browser details, request times and delivery or security logs to operate and protect the portal, investigate problems and limit abuse.

When we use and share personal data

We collect, use and share personal data for the purposes explained here and at the point of collection, with your consent where required or as the law otherwise allows. If we need to use or share your data for a new purpose, we will tell you first and ask for consent when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

  • Groups and organisers. Group members can see their roster, task progress and shared evidence. Evidence is not posted publicly by the portal. Anyone with a valid invitation link can see the inviter’s name and group name before joining, so share those links privately. Authorised organisers can see account, group and event records to manage access and review submissions.
  • Service providers. Cloudflare hosts the portal and stores its records and evidence. Google provides optional Google sign-in and may receive a submitted Maps short link when we look up its pin. The configured email provider (Gmail or Resend) processes recipient addresses and message contents. If you enable device alerts, your browser or device’s push service handles the subscription and delivers alerts. A caught alert can appear on your lock screen.
  • Legal disclosures. We may disclose data to authorities or other parties when required by law or as the PDPA otherwise permits.

Some service providers may process data outside Singapore. Before transferring personal data overseas, we must ensure it receives protection comparable to the PDPA, in accordance with Singapore’s transfer rules.

We use and transfer information received from Google APIs in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google sign-in data for advertising or to train AI models. If Gmail is used to send messages, only the organiser’s sender account grants the portal permission to send email.

Cookies and browser storage

The portal uses cookies to keep you signed in and secure sign-in, verification and recovery. Your browser may also save an invitation while you sign in, remember where to return after Google sign-in, or remember which account uses device notifications. The portal does not use these tools for advertising or analytics. Blocking or clearing site storage can affect sign-in and features that depend on it.

How long we keep data and how we protect it

We keep personal data only while it is needed for the purpose we collected it for or for a legal or business need, such as resolving event disputes, preventing abuse or restoring the service. When those needs end, we delete it or remove the details that can identify you. Removing an account can clear sign-in and contact details while leaving event history and shared evidence in place. That evidence may still identify you. Contact us if you want us to review a specific record or upload.

We use safeguards such as protected sign-in credentials, private evidence storage and access checks. If there is a data breach, we will assess it and notify the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) and affected people when the PDPA requires us to do so.

Your choices and requests

In Settings, you can update some account details, manage contact addresses and turn off device notifications. You can also write to us to:

  • Access or correct your data. You can ask for personal data about you that we hold or control, information about how it was or may have been used or disclosed in the year before your request, or a correction to an error or omission. The PDPA provides for some exceptions. We will handle your request promptly. If we cannot respond to an access or correction request within 30 days, we will write to you within that time to tell you when we can respond.
  • Withdraw consent. Tell us what you want us to stop doing and give us reasonable notice. We will explain the likely effects. For example, we may be unable to continue providing an account or feature that needs the data. We and those handling the data for us will stop collecting, using or sharing it for that purpose unless the law requires or allows us to continue.
  • Raise a concern or request removal. You can report a privacy concern, make a complaint or ask us to review whether data or a particular upload can be removed. Withdrawal does not automatically delete records we still need to keep under the retention rules above.

Children and event participation

If you are under 13, ask your parent or legal guardian to contact us before you sign up or send us personal data. Wait until we explain what data we need and why, and confirm their consent. If you are 13 or older and don’t understand what giving or withdrawing consent means, ask a parent or guardian for help. Where a young person cannot give informed consent, we need it from their parent or legal guardian first.

Before uploading evidence, leave out bystanders and personal details that the task does not need. If the evidence identifies someone else, explain who can see it and get their permission before sharing. If it identifies a child under 13, contact us first and do not upload it until we confirm consent from their parent or legal guardian. Parents, guardians and anyone shown in evidence can contact us about an upload.

Data protection contact and complaints

For the attention of the Manhunt IX Data Protection Officer, email xyz2011a@gmail.com. Describe your request and, if relevant, give your username or identify the upload. Please don’t send passwords, identity documents or a child’s personal details in your first message. We may ask for enough information to verify who you are or confirm you can act for someone else. If you still have a concern after contacting us, you can raise it with Singapore’s PDPC.

Changes to this policy

We will post updates here and let affected users know about material changes to how we handle their personal data. We will ask for consent to new purposes where required.
